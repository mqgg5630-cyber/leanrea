## 公共块（每页原样复制，与批A/批B一致，保证角色一致）

### CHARACTER
```
CHARACTER 1 (white panda cub, "Yi'er"): chubby pure-white round body and round head,
two solid very dark brown round ears on top of the head, small dark brown oval eyes,
tiny mouth, soft pink round blush cheeks, a small dark brown bow tie at the neck,
short white stubby arms, small dark brown feet.
CHARACTER 2 (milk-tea brown bear cub, "Bubu"): slightly bigger chubby milk-tea brown
round body and round head, small round ears with darker brown inner ear, small black
dot eyes, tiny nose, pale yellow round blush cheeks, short stubby limbs, no scarf,
no accessories.
```

### STYLE
```
Style: thick dark brown outlines, completely flat coloring, warm pastel interior
background, soft even lighting, no gradients, no shading, no texture, simple
rounded shapes, cozy warm healing mood, vertical portrait 3:4, one single
continuous scene, the illustration fills the entire frame edge to edge, no panel
borders, no white frame.
```

### TWO-PANEL（内容页）
```
The illustration is a comic page split into TWO stacked panels separated by a
clean straight horizontal edge: the UPPER panel is bright and warmly lit, the
LOWER panel is dimmer and muted/greyish. No panel borders, no frames, no white
margin.
```

### NO-TEXT（每页原样复制）
```
CRITICAL: absolutely no text, no Chinese characters, no letters, no numbers, no
captions, no speech balloons, no speech bubbles, no watermark, no logo, no signature.
```

## 逐页 SCENE

**C1 封面（单幅，非分格）**
```
At a warm sunset park path, the white panda cub and the brown bear cub walk side
by side with a small comfortable gap between them, both relaxed and smiling, a few
round trees, a lamppost, golden sunlight, full body, front view.
```

**C2 见面频率**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright living room, the white panda cub holds a small wall calendar
and marks three circles on it with a crayon, looking a little unsure, the brown bear
cub sits on the sofa watching calmly, a cup on the table. LOWER PANEL: same room
dimmer muted greyish, the brown bear cub opens a shared calendar notebook on the
table and points at it with one paw, the white panda cub leans closer to look,
relaxed mood.
```

**C3 独处**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright cozy room, the white panda cub sits in the far corner of the
sofa hugging a pillow and looking across at the brown bear cub, who reads a book at
a small table, a little distance between them, warm window light. LOWER PANEL: same
room dimmer muted greyish, the brown bear cub keeps reading and lifts one paw in a
gentle small wave, the panda smiles and sinks back into the pillow.
```

**C4 身体边界**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright bedroom doorway, the white panda cub stands close to the brown
bear cub with a slightly unsure tilted head, the brown bear cub holds one open paw
raised in the air and waits, not touching, both gentle and calm, warm light.
LOWER PANEL: same room dimmer muted greyish, the brown bear cub has stepped back and
sits on the soft rug, the white panda cub relaxes and smiles, safe cozy mood.
```

**C5 说偏好**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright bedroom with a soft rug, the two cubs sit facing each other
with a small gap, the white panda cub looks shy and fidgety with paws together, the
brown bear cub listens with a kind neutral face, a small folded paper card on the
rug between them. LOWER PANEL: same room dimmer muted greyish, the brown bear cub
slides the small paper card toward the panda with one paw, the panda holds it and
smiles, cozy mood.
```

**C6 撤退时刻**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright bedroom at night, the white panda cub sits on the edge of the
bed hugging a blanket and looking down quietly, the brown bear cub stands nearby
holding a glass of water, warm lamp light. LOWER PANEL: same room dimmer muted
greyish, the brown bear cub places the glass of water on the bedside table and walks
a couple of steps away to give space, the panda looks up a little, calmer.
```

**C7 异地**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright small room, the white panda cub sits by the window holding a
phone to its ear, a wall calendar beside the window, warm light. LOWER PANEL: A
different small room, dimmer muted greyish, the brown bear cub holds a phone and
smiles gently, a small desk calendar and a mug on the desk, quiet evening mood.
```

**C8 家人边界**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright apartment entryway, the white panda cub stands beside the
brown bear cub near the front door, a pair of visitor shoes on the floor by the
door, both looking at each other, warm light. LOWER PANEL: same entryway dimmer
muted greyish, the two cubs stand shoulder to shoulder facing the closed door, the
brown bear cub rests one gentle paw on the panda's back, both calm, a small key on
a table.
```

**C9 钱的规则**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright kitchen table, the white panda cub holds a paper bill
hesitantly in both paws, the brown bear cub sits opposite, a small notebook and a
piggy bank on the table, warm light. LOWER PANEL: same table dimmer muted greyish,
the brown bear cub writes three short lines in the small notebook with a pencil and
slides it toward the panda, the panda looks at the notebook and nods, calm mood.
```

**C10 尾页 靠近的节奏**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright park path in the afternoon, the white panda cub walks a few
steps ahead holding a small paper lantern, the brown bear cub walks behind at a
comfortable distance, both relaxed, round trees, warm light. LOWER PANEL: same path
dimmer muted greyish, the two cubs sit together on a bench with a small comfortable
gap, the panda smiles, the bear holds a small thermos, evening sky.
```
