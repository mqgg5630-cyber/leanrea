## 公共块（每页原样复制，与批A完全一致，保证角色一致）

### CHARACTER
```
CHARACTER 1 (white panda cub, "Yi'er"): chubby pure-white round body and round head,
two solid very dark brown round ears on top of the head, small dark brown oval eyes,
tiny mouth, soft pink round blush cheeks, a small dark brown bow tie at the neck,
short white stubby arms, small dark brown feet.
CHARACTER 2 (milk-tea brown bear cub, "Bubu"): slightly bigger chubby milk-tea brown
round body and round head, small round ears with darker brown inner ear, small black
dot eyes, tiny nose, pale yellow round blush cheeks, short stubby limbs, no scarf,
no accessories.
```

### STYLE
```
Style: thick dark brown outlines, completely flat coloring, warm pastel interior
background, soft even lighting, no gradients, no shading, no texture, simple
rounded shapes, cozy warm healing mood, vertical portrait 3:4, one single
continuous scene, the illustration fills the entire frame edge to edge, no panel
borders, no white frame.
```

### TWO-PANEL（内容页）
```
The illustration is a comic page split into TWO stacked panels separated by a
clean straight horizontal edge: the UPPER panel is bright and warmly lit, the
LOWER panel is dimmer and muted/greyish. No panel borders, no frames, no white
margin.
```

### NO-TEXT（每页原样复制）
```
CRITICAL: absolutely no text, no Chinese characters, no letters, no numbers, no
captions, no speech balloons, no speech bubbles, no watermark, no logo, no signature.
```

## 逐页 SCENE

**B1 封面（单幅，非分格）**
```
At a warm golden-hour crossroads, the white panda cub walks away along the path with
a small backpack on its back, turning back to wave, the brown bear cub stands in the
doorway of a small house watching it go, soft trees and bushes, warm sunlight, full
body, front view.
```

**B2 先分清**
```
UPPER PANEL: A dim evening living room, a messy table with scattered papers and cups,
the white panda cub sits at the table with its head down and shoulders slumped, a dark
window behind. LOWER PANEL: same room dimmer and muted greyish, the brown bear cub
quietly slides a warm cup of tea across the table toward the panda and waits with
paws folded, the panda looks up.
```

**B3 不是你的错**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright room, the white panda cub sits on the floor looking down at its
own paws, a crumpled paper ball on the floor beside it, a soft rug. LOWER PANEL: same
room dimmer muted greyish, the brown bear cub picks the crumpled paper up and drops
it into a small bin, standing beside the panda with one gentle open paw, the panda
looks up.
```

**B4 一次讲清**
```
UPPER PANEL: A small round table with a teapot and two cups, the two cubs sit facing
each other, the white panda cub has one paw raised while talking, the brown bear cub
listens. LOWER PANEL: same table dimmer muted greyish, the brown bear cub leans
forward with both paws on the table and head slightly bowed listening, the white panda
cub has finished speaking, a quiet calm mood.
```

**B5 沉默也是回答**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright bedroom, the white panda cub sits on the bed holding a smartphone
with a blank lit screen, staring at it and waiting, evening light. LOWER PANEL: same
room dimmer muted greyish, the white panda cub places the phone face down on the bed
and exhales with eyes closed, the brown bear cub sits next to it with a small nod.
```

**B6 不替他决定**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright doorway in the rain, the white panda cub stands at the threshold
holding a closed umbrella, pausing and looking out. LOWER PANEL: same doorway dimmer
muted greyish, the white panda cub turns away and walks off with the umbrella folded,
the brown bear cub stands far away near a tree watching without following.
```

**B7 照顾你自己**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright living room floor, the white panda cub sits on the floor wrapped
in a small blanket, knees up, tired, a cushion nearby. LOWER PANEL: same room dimmer
muted greyish warm light, the brown bear cub pulls the blanket up over the panda's
shoulders and sits down beside it, both calm.
```

**B8 引导者也会累**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright living room sofa, the brown bear cub lies back alone on the sofa
looking tired and drained, empty room, the white panda cub not visible. LOWER PANEL:
same sofa dimmer muted greyish warm light, the two cubs sit side by side on the sofa
with their shoulders touching, both relaxed.
```

**B9 别等崩了才求助**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright room with a round rug, the two cubs sit facing each other on the
rug, both looking a bit tired, a small clock and a mug between them. LOWER PANEL: a
waiting area dimmer muted greyish, the two cubs sit side by side on a bench, the brown
bear cub holding a small notebook, a potted plant beside them.
```

**B10 尾页**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright doorway, the white panda cub stands at the door holding a small
bag, the brown bear cub stands opposite facing it, a warm home interior behind. LOWER
PANEL: same doorway dimmer muted greyish warm evening light, the brown bear cub gently
pulls the door half closed, the two cubs look at each other through the narrowing gap,
warm lamp light inside.
```
