## 公共块（每页原样复制）

### CHARACTER
```
CHARACTER 1 (white panda cub, "Yi'er"): chubby pure-white round body and round head,
two solid very dark brown round ears on top of the head, small dark brown oval eyes,
tiny mouth, soft pink round blush cheeks, a small dark brown bow tie at the neck,
short white stubby arms, small dark brown feet.
CHARACTER 2 (milk-tea brown bear cub, "Bubu"): slightly bigger chubby milk-tea brown
round body and round head, small round ears with darker brown inner ear, small black
dot eyes, tiny nose, pale yellow round blush cheeks, short stubby limbs, no scarf,
no accessories.
```

### STYLE
```
Style: thick dark brown outlines, completely flat coloring, warm pastel interior
background, soft even lighting, no gradients, no shading, no texture, simple
rounded shapes, cozy warm healing mood, vertical portrait 3:4, one single
continuous scene, the illustration fills the entire frame edge to edge, no panel
borders, no white frame.
```

### TWO-PANEL（内容页）
```
The illustration is a comic page split into TWO stacked panels separated by a
clean straight horizontal edge: the UPPER panel is bright and warmly lit, the
LOWER panel is dimmer and muted/greyish. No panel borders, no frames, no white
margin.
```

### NO-TEXT（每页原样复制）
```
CRITICAL: absolutely no text, no Chinese characters, no letters, no numbers, no
captions, no speech balloons, no speech bubbles, no watermark, no logo, no signature.
```

## 逐页 SCENE

**D1 封面（单幅，非分格）**
```
At a warm cozy room, the white panda cub and the brown bear cub sit facing each
other across a small round table with two cups, the panda talking with one paw
raised while the bear listens with head slightly tilted, soft window light, a
plant in the corner, full body, front view.
```

**D2 四个动作**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright living room, the white panda cub talks eagerly with one paw
raised, a small stack of blank paper cards on the table, the brown bear cub holds
one open paw up as if asking to wait and looks a little tired. LOWER PANEL: same
room dimmer muted greyish, the brown bear cub has pushed the stack of cards aside
and leans forward listening with both paws on the table, the white panda cub
calmer now, talking with a small smile.
```

**D3 一次一问**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright living room, the white panda cub looks overwhelmed with both
paws up and mouth open, the brown bear cub leans in with one paw raised, asking.
LOWER PANEL: same room dimmer muted greyish, the brown bear cub raises exactly one
paw and waits with a patient smile, the white panda cub takes a breath, one finger
raised, thinking calmly.
```

**D4 两个选项**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright kitchen table, a small bowl of noodles and a tiny straw hat
lie on the table, the white panda cub gestures at the brown bear cub questioningly,
the bear looks uncertain. LOWER PANEL: same table dimmer muted greyish, the brown
bear cub slides two small blank cards toward the panda with one paw, the panda
looks at the two cards and touches one of them, the bear smiles.
```

**D5 还回去**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright room, the brown bear cub thinks with a paw on its chin, two
small blank cards lie on the table, the white panda cub pushes the cards toward
the bear. LOWER PANEL: same room dimmer muted greyish, the brown bear cub slides
the two cards back to the panda with both paws, the panda picks one card up and
looks at it, the bear watches calmly.
```

**D6 先复述**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright bedroom, the white panda cub sits on the bed talking with
animated gestures, the brown bear cub sits nearby facing them and listening
carefully with head slightly tilted, a cup of tea untouched on the table. LOWER
PANEL: same room dimmer muted greyish, the bear has an open paw raised in a calm
mirroring gesture, the panda's shoulders drop and its expression softens, both
calm.
```

**D7 夸具体行为**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright apartment hallway, the white panda cub just came in through
the door, the brown bear cub greets them with a warm smile, the panda shrugs with a
slightly dismissive tilted head. LOWER PANEL: same hallway dimmer muted greyish,
the brown bear cub lifts one paw in a small approving gesture, the white panda cub
blushes and smiles shyly, a small clock on the wall.
```

**D8 不贴标签**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright room, the white panda cub stands with arms crossed and an
annoyed expression, the brown bear cub holds a thick blank book and starts to open
it. LOWER PANEL: same room dimmer muted greyish, the brown bear cub has closed the
book and put it on a shelf, instead pointing gently at a small suitcase standing
by the door, the white panda cub looks at the suitcase and starts to talk, relaxed.
```

**D9 换方法**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright living room, the white panda cub talks with both paws raised
and a frustrated expression, the brown bear cub looks weary, one paw on its head.
LOWER PANEL: same room dimmer muted greyish, the brown bear cub raises an open paw
to pause, the panda pauses, three small blank round tokens lie on the table between
them, the bear points at them one by one, the panda thinks.
```

**D10 尾页 留白**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright evening park bench, the two cubs sit side by side with a
comfortable gap, the white panda cub talks softly with one paw, the brown bear cub
listens, a small paper lantern beside them. LOWER PANEL: same bench dimmer muted
greyish, the two cubs sit quietly without talking, both looking up at the evening
sky, two cups on the bench, calm and quiet.
```
