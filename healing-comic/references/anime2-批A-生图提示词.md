## 公共块（每页原样复制）

### CHARACTER
```
CHARACTER 1 (white panda cub, "Yi'er"): chubby pure-white round body and round head,
two solid very dark brown round ears on top of the head, small dark brown oval eyes,
tiny mouth, soft pink round blush cheeks, a small dark brown bow tie at the neck,
short white stubby arms, small dark brown feet.
CHARACTER 2 (milk-tea brown bear cub, "Bubu"): slightly bigger chubby milk-tea brown
round body and round head, small round ears with darker brown inner ear, small black
dot eyes, tiny nose, pale yellow round blush cheeks, short stubby limbs, no scarf,
no accessories.
```

### STYLE
```
Style: thick dark brown outlines, completely flat coloring, warm pastel interior
background, soft even lighting, no gradients, no shading, no texture, simple
rounded shapes, cozy warm healing mood, vertical portrait 3:4, one single
continuous scene, the illustration fills the entire frame edge to edge, no panel
borders, no white frame.
```

### TWO-PANEL（内容页）
```
The illustration is a comic page split into TWO stacked panels separated by a
clean straight horizontal edge: the UPPER panel is bright and warmly lit, the
LOWER panel is dimmer and muted/greyish. No panel borders, no frames, no white
margin.
```

### NO-TEXT（每页原样复制）
```
CRITICAL: absolutely no text, no Chinese characters, no letters, no numbers, no
captions, no speech balloons, no speech bubbles, no watermark, no logo, no signature.
```

## 逐页 SCENE

**P1 封面（单幅，非分格）**
```
At a small quiet crossroads on a warm sunny afternoon, the two cubs stand side by
side in front of a small wooden signpost with two blank arrow boards pointing in
different directions, the boards are completely blank with no writing. The white
panda cub looks up at the signpost, the brown bear cub glances at the panda. Soft
green bushes, a cream colored wall and warm sky behind. Full body, front view.
```

**P2 一句话检验**
```
UPPER PANEL: Cozy living room in bright daylight. The white panda cub sits curled
up on the corner of a soft sofa hugging a round pillow, shoulders slightly hunched,
looking small and hesitant. The brown bear cub stands one step away with open
relaxed paws, not touching, giving space. LOWER PANEL: same room in dimmer muted
greyish evening light. The brown bear cub has stepped back to the doorway with an
open palm leaving a wide gap, the white panda cub lifts its head and looks at the
bear with slightly brighter eyes. Soft rug and a small side table.
```

**P3 以爱之名**
```
UPPER PANEL: A small bright studio corner with an easel and paint jars. The brown
bear cub is putting a box of brushes and paints away into a wooden crate, the white
panda cub stands beside it with ears up, puzzled, one paw half raised. Art supplies
on a low table. LOWER PANEL: same corner in dimmer muted greyish light. The white
panda cub takes a brush back out of the box and holds it up, the brown bear cub
stands with both paws open in a friendly shrug and a small smile, the easel behind.
```

**P4 手机这一关**
```
UPPER PANEL: A small wooden dining table in bright daylight. The white panda cub
pushes a smartphone face down onto the table and looks up with a serious face, the
brown bear cub leans in slightly with eyes on the phone and a guilty expression.
LOWER PANEL: same table in dimmer muted greyish light. The brown bear cub slides
the phone back across the table toward the panda with an open palm and a lowered
head, the panda looks at the phone without reaching for it yet.
```

**P5 不盯梢**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright entryway. The white panda cub stands in the open doorway
with a small shoulder bag, turning back to look into the room, the brown bear cub
stands inside near the shoe rack with one paw half lifted but staying in place.
LOWER PANEL: same entryway in dimmer muted greyish evening light. The brown bear
cub sits inside by the door with a cup of tea, relaxed, looking out through the
open door and staying put, the white panda cub is a small silhouette at the far
edge of the doorway.
```

**P6 两个选项**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright kitchen counter with two tall glasses of different colored
drinks side by side, one pale orange and one pale green. The white panda cub
stands in front of them pointing with one paw from one glass to the other,
thinking hard. LOWER PANEL: same counter in dimmer muted greyish light. The brown
bear cub stands with both paws behind its back smiling and waiting, letting the
panda choose, the white panda cub now holding the pale green glass close to its
chest.
```

**P7 只谈行为**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright desk with paper and pencils. The two cubs sit side by side at
a small round table, each writing on a sheet of paper, both looking down, calm.
LOWER PANEL: same table in dimmer muted greyish light. The two cubs swap the two
sheets of paper and read them, the panda pointing at one line, the bear listening
with a small nod.
```

**P8 不当救世主**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright stairwell landing with a tall window. The white panda cub
sits alone on the bottom step hugging its knees and looking down, sunlight from the
window, empty space around. LOWER PANEL: same stairwell in dimmer muted greyish
light. The brown bear cub sits down next to the panda on the same step with a
little gap between them, holding a phone in both paws with the screen facing up,
the panda leans slightly toward the bear.
```

**P9 危险信号**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright bedroom. The white panda cub stands up abruptly with fists
clenched at its sides, eyebrows drawn and mouth tight, body tense but not violent,
the brown bear cub steps forward with both paws raised open, calm and careful.
LOWER PANEL: the dimmer muted greyish entryway of the same home. The two cubs stand
together at the open door, the brown bear cub's paw resting on the door frame, the
panda one step behind, both calmer, breathing room around them.
```

**P10 尾页**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright window corner in late afternoon, a big window with warm light
and a small plant on the sill. The two cubs stand side by side facing the window,
the panda's shoulder touching the bear's arm. LOWER PANEL: same window corner in
dimmer muted greyish warm evening light with a small lamp switched on. The white
panda cub hands the round pillow over to the brown bear cub, who takes it with both
paws and smiles softly, both standing close together.
```
