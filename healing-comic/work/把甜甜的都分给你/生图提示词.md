## 公共块（每页原样复制）

### CHARACTER
```
CHARACTER 1 (white panda cub, "Yi'er"): chubby pure-white round body and round head,
two solid very dark brown round ears on top of the head, small dark brown oval eyes,
tiny mouth, soft pink round blush cheeks, a small dark brown bow tie at the neck,
short white stubby arms, small dark brown feet.
CHARACTER 2 (milk-tea brown bear cub, "Bubu"): slightly bigger chubby milk-tea brown
round body and round head, small round ears with darker brown inner ear, small black
dot eyes, tiny nose, pale yellow round blush cheeks, short stubby limbs, no scarf,
no accessories.
```

### STYLE
```
Style: thick dark brown outlines, completely flat coloring, warm pastel interior
background, soft even lighting, no gradients, no shading, no texture, simple
rounded shapes, cozy warm healing mood, vertical portrait 3:4, one single
continuous scene, the illustration fills the entire frame edge to edge, no panel
borders, no white frame.
```

### TWO-PANEL（内容页）
```
The illustration is a comic page split into TWO stacked panels separated by a
clean straight horizontal edge: the UPPER panel is bright and warmly lit, the
LOWER panel is dimmer and muted/greyish. No panel borders, no frames, no white
margin.
```

### NO-TEXT（每页原样复制）
```
CRITICAL: absolutely no text, no Chinese characters, no letters, no numbers, no
captions, no speech balloons, no speech bubbles, no watermark, no logo, no signature.
```

### 气泡留位（写进每个内容页 SCENE 结尾）
```
Keep both characters small and low-center inside each panel, with clean simple
background at the top of the upper panel and clean floor at the lower right of
the lower panel, so speech bubbles can be added later.
```

## 逐页 SCENE

**P1 封面（单幅，非分格）**
```
On a small round grassy hill under a soft cream and peach sunset sky, the two cubs
sit side by side low-center in the frame. The white panda cub holds up ONE big red
strawberry between them with both stubby arms; the brown bear cub leans gently
toward it with happy closed eyes. A tiny woven basket with two more strawberries
rests on the grass beside them, a few small round clouds in the sky. Large clean
empty soft cream sky across the top third of the frame for later text overlay.
Full bodies visible, front view.
```

**P2 一人一半**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright cozy kitchen in daytime. The white panda cub stands at a
small wooden table, gently pushing a little plate with ONE last red strawberry
toward the brown bear cub, ears slightly drooping, generous but reluctant. The
brown bear cub looks down at the plate, surprised. LOWER PANEL: the same kitchen
in dimmer muted greyish evening light. The brown bear cub holds HALF of the
strawberry out to the white panda cub's mouth with one paw, smiling softly; the
white panda cub leans in with round happy eyes. Characters slightly left of
center, clean floor to the right.
```

**P3 雨天来接**
```
UPPER PANEL: A rainy afternoon street. The white panda cub stands alone under a
cream shop awning, holding a tiny shoulder bag, watching big round raindrops and
puddles, ears drooping. LOWER PANEL: the same street in dimmer muted grey-blue
evening light. The two cubs walk slowly side by side under ONE big yellow
umbrella; the umbrella is clearly tilted toward the white panda cub's side, the
brown bear cub's round shoulder is getting wet, both smiling gently. Characters
low-center, clean wet street to the right.
```

**P4 生病照顾**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright bedroom in daytime. The white panda cub lies in bed with a
blanket pulled up over its ears, only round worried eyes showing, a small round
thermometer on the side table. LOWER PANEL: the same bedroom in dimmer muted
greyish warm evening light with a small lamp glowing. The brown bear cub sits on
a little stool by the bed, offering a steaming bowl of porridge with both paws,
smiling patiently; the white panda cub peeks out of the blanket looking at the
bowl. Characters low-center, clean wall to the right.
```

**P5 睡不着**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bedroom in the evening. The white panda cub lies in bed with wide
open eyes, paws resting on its round belly, counting sheep; soft moonlight through
a round window. LOWER PANEL: the same bedroom in dimmer muted greyish warm light.
The brown bear cub sits on the floor beside the bed, one paw softly patting the
blanket in a slow rhythm, eyes half closed and humming; the white panda cub's eyes
are drifting shut. Characters low-center, clean floor to the right.
```

**P6 煮糊的面**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright kitchen in daytime. The white panda cub stands holding a
wooden spoon with both paws, looking guilty at a small pot of slightly dark
overcooked noodles, tiny wisp of steam rising, ears drooping. LOWER PANEL: the
same kitchen in dimmer muted greyish evening light. The two cubs sit together at
a small round table happily eating the noodles from ONE big bowl, the brown bear
cub slurping with full round cheeks, the white panda cub relieved and smiling.
Characters low-center, clean wall to the right.
```

**P7 想哭就哭**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright park bench in the afternoon. The white panda cub sits on
the bench with teary round eyes, face turned slightly away, chin trembling,
paws gripping the bench edge. LOWER PANEL: the same park in dimmer muted greyish
warm dusk light. The brown bear cub sits close beside it, holding out a small
folded handkerchief with both paws; the white panda cub reaches one paw toward
the handkerchief, their round heads close together. Characters low-center, clean
path to the right.
```

**P8 口袋取暖**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright winter street with soft falling snow. The white panda cub
stands blowing warm breath onto its small paws, cheeks extra pink, ears drooping
from the cold. LOWER PANEL: the same street in dimmer muted greyish evening light
under one glowing round street lamp. The brown bear cub cups the white panda
cub's paws inside its own round paws, both standing very close, snowflakes
around them, both smiling gently. Characters low-center, clean snowy ground to
the right.
```

**P9 留灯等你**
```
UPPER PANEL: A late afternoon city sidewalk. The white panda cub walks alone with
a tiny shoulder bag, looking up at a sky turning dark, small round clouds, a few
first stars. LOWER PANEL: a cozy home interior at night in dimmer muted warm
light. Seen through the warm glowing window of a small house: the brown bear cub
stands by the stove stirring a little pot, a round lamp on, the table set with
two bowls. Characters low-center, clean night sky to the right.
```

**P10 尾页 · 明天也是**
```
UPPER PANEL: A bright window corner at golden sunset. The two cubs stand side by
```
